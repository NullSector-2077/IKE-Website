BUILT WITH INTENTION

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Protection, productivity and control come together in one considered browsing experience.

⌁

Protection that adapts

Five protection levels, from Compatibility to Maximum. Set them globally or tailor them to individual sites.

◌

Less noise. More web.

Built-in ad and tracker blocking, cosmetic filters and automatic cookie-banner handling.

▤

Your browsing, organised

Workspaces, containers, tab groups, split view and vertical tabs help keep your work in order.

▣

Private by design

A local password vault, private windows, site permissions and clear controls for your data.

⌘

Make it yours

Custom themes, toolbar controls, user scripts and a new-tab page you can shape to your needs.

◫

Tools within reach

PDF viewer, reader mode, translation, picture-in-picture, downloads and page monitoring.

MADE TO BE YOURS

A browser with a point of view.
And room for yours.

I.K.E. is an independent project built with Python and Qt. It uses Qt WebEngine, powered by Chromium and provided by the operating system on Arch-based systems.
